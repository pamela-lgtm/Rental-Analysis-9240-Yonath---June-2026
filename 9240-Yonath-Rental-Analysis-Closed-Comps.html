Why It Leases Here

Positioning rationale

Working in the property's favor

  • The plan sells itself. The Reef is one of Laureate Park's most recognized floor plans — relocating tenants often know it by name.
  • Half-bath edge & size. 3.5 baths and 2,924 sq ft both exceed the closest closed comp, supporting a premium over its $3,840.
  • Peak leasing season. A late-June launch lands in Lake Nona's strongest relocation and school-timing window.
  • Medical City demand. Proximity to UCF/VA hospitals, Nemours, KPMG, and the Town Center keeps qualified-tenant velocity high.

Confirm at walkthrough

  • Garage configuration — a 3-car (vs 2-car) supports the top of the range.
  • Outdoor living — screened lanai, pool, or premium hardscape would lift positioning toward $4,100.
  • Finish level — quartz, 42" cabinetry, and flooring relative to show-ready standard.
  • What's included — lawn care and the community 1 Gig fiber shift the net comparison and the marketing hook.
The Verandah View

The right benchmark is the cost of vacancy

Pricing is never about chasing the highest possible number — it is about the shortest path to the right tenant at a strong rate. The closed data here is unambiguous: well-priced Laureate Park homes lease in under a week, while every home reaching for a premium it can't support sits for months. One vacant month erases far more than the difference an over-ask ever recovers.

Condition is the lever. A property presented to show-ready standard photographs better, shows better, and earns the upper edge of its range — which is exactly where this analysis places 9240 Yonath Street: priced to lease quickly, to a qualified tenant, at a number the market has already proven it will pay.
